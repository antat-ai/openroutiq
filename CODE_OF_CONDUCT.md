# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior includes respectful technical disagreement, constructive feedback, empathy, clear attribution, and attention to user safety. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub profiles or repository moderation channels. Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, subject to safety and legal obligations.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
